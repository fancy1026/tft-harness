# Patch 18.4 changes (2026-10-07)

The other game-data files are from patch 18.3 data. Where they disagree with this list, this
list is current. Format: old => new.

- Combat arrival and combat departure phases: 1 second shorter each.

- Sprykin (7) stats: 45% HP + 45% AS => 55% HP + 55% AS

## Wisps
- Polymorph Wisps are only offered in the first 2 seconds of a planning phase. Greater
  Polymorph is re-enabled.
- Not offered below 20 HP: stat consumables (Knickknacks, Thingamajigs, Doodads), Golden Goose,
  Major Gambit, Barter.
- New 0-cost Wisps (stage 4-1 to 5-7, only offered when you have under 10 gold):
  - Drizzle: after 8 s of combat your team gains 10 mana (upgraded: 15)
  - Grove Armor: your highest-health champion gains 15 armor and 15 MR (upgraded: 25/25)
  - Returning Friend: a temporary Training Dummy with two Giant's Belts (upgraded: three)
  - Room for a Shroom: 2 random mushrooms (upgraded: 3)
- Backrow Star AS: 85% => 100% (upgraded 115% => 130%)
- Forest Guide cost: 5 => 6 gold (upgraded 4 => 5)
- Giant's Growth HP: 750 => 800 (upgraded 750 => 800)
- Improved Reach cost: 2 => 1 gold (upgraded 2 => 1)
- Killing Frenzy AS on kill: 115% => 130% (upgraded 140% => 160%)
- Mana Potion mana restored 80 => 70, starting mana 20 => 10 (radiant 90 => 80, 20 => 10)
- Stealthy duration: 6 => 8 s (upgraded 10 => 12 s); affects your 3 strongest Assassins or
  Fighters

## Bug fixes that change play
- Bramble Vest max health is now additive with other max-health increases.
- A unit could not be sold while it sat in the bench slot a just-starred-up champion had left:
  fixed.
- Buying a 2/3-star-completing champion with a full bench: improved.
